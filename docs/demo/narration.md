## Notes for HITL Voice-Over Artist

- **Pace**: Speak at a natural conversational pace. The timings above assume normal speech; if you speak faster, the cues will drift. Use the on-screen action (not the clock) as your guide — match the narration to what you see.
- **Tone**: Professional, matter-of-fact. No marketing hype. The product proves itself on screen; you're just describing what's happening.
- **Scene 3.2 hold**: This is the power moment. When you finish the narration at [03:00], **stop and count silently to 6 while the hook error fills the screen.** The silence and the red error together are the governance proof. Don't fill it with "and so you see..." — let it breathe.
- **Closing CTA**: End with energy; this is the call to action. Pause for 1–2 seconds before closing if the recording allows.
