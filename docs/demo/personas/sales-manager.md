# Sales Manager Persona

**Role**: Internal sales representative or customer success manager handling quote negotiation and deal closure.

**Pain Points**:
- Managing multiple customer quotes with different terms and timelines
- No centralized communication history — context is scattered across emails
- Difficulty tracking which deals are in negotiation vs. ready to close
- Manual pricing updates create lag and errors

**Platform Needs**:
- View all customer quote requests with metadata (company, value, status)
- Send counter-offers directly in the platform (price, terms, delivery)
- See full message history for each deal
- Manage approval workflow with company admins

**Relevant Flows**:

| Flow | Title | Outcome |
|------|-------|---------|
| 02 | Approval | Sales manager receives approval from customer admin; can close deal |
| 05 | Quote Negotiation | Sales manager negotiates pricing; both parties see full trail |
