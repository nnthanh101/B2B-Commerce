# Buyer Persona

**Role**: Procurement officer, field engineer, or operations lead at a customer company.

**Pain Points**:
- Cannot self-serve large or regulated purchases without manager approval
- Manually tracking budgets across multiple teams
- SKU lookups take time — needs fast re-order capability
- Communication delays when negotiating bulk pricing

**Platform Needs**:
- Browse catalog and add items to cart
- Request quotes instead of direct checkout (deferred approval)
- See real-time spending limits and remaining budget
- Negotiate pricing via quote messages
- Bulk import and quick-entry order options

**Relevant Flows**:

| Flow | Title | Outcome |
|------|-------|---------|
| 01 | Cart to Quote | Buyer initiates RFQ; admin receives it for review |
| 04 | Spending Limit Enforcement | Budget controls prevent overspend; exceptions are tracked |
| 05 | Quote Negotiation | Buyer and seller exchange messages; both see full history |
| 06 | Promotions | Bulk and time-based discounts auto-apply; buyer sees savings |
| 07 | Full E-Commerce | Buyer browses, adds items, checks out end-to-end |
| 09 | Bulk Add to Cart | Multiple items imported from list in seconds |
| 10 | Quick Order Pad | Fast re-orders using known SKUs |
