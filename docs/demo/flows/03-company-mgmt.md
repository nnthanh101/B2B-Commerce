# Flow 03: Company Management

**Persona**: Admin  
**Scenario**: A company administrator manages team members and their purchase permissions.  
**Duration**: ~2 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "Company management is where administrators control who can buy on behalf of the organization."

**[00:09]** "We see the company profile: Demo Corp, located in Auckland, currency set to NZD."

**[00:16]** "The Employees tab lists all team members linked to this company. We see the buyer from earlier, plus other procurement staff."

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:24]** "Each employee has a spending limit. For example, the buyer has a 50,000 NZD monthly limit. Team leads get higher limits; junior staff get lower ones."

**[00:35]** "An admin can invite new team members by email. The system sends an invite link; they register and are automatically enrolled."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:45]** "Company management ensures accountability: we know who's ordering what, and we can enforce budget controls per person."

**[00:53]** "Company management complete."
