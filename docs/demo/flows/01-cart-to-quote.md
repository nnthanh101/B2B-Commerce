# Flow 01: Cart to Quote

**Persona**: Buyer  
**Scenario**: A procurement officer needs to request a quote for bulk office electronics before purchasing.  
**Duration**: ~2 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "This is cart-to-quote. We're logged in as a buyer. Notice the storefront shows products available for B2B purchase."

**[00:08]** "We add a laptop, monitor, and keyboard to the cart. Each item shows its unit price."

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:15]** "The cart summary shows the subtotal. Our spending limit is 50,000 — we're well below that."

**[00:22]** "Instead of checking out directly, we click Request Quote. This tells the system: don't charge yet, just prepare the order for approval."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:32]** "The system creates a quote record. We see the quote number and status: Draft. The quote is ready for our admin team to review and decide on pricing."

**[00:42]** "Cart-to-quote complete. The buyer initiated the workflow; the admin receives a notification."
