# Flow 07: Full E-Commerce (Browse, Cart, Checkout)

**Persona**: Buyer  
**Scenario**: A buyer discovers products, adds them to cart, and checks out in a unified storefront experience.  
**Duration**: ~2.5 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "Full e-commerce starts with discovery. The buyer lands on the storefront and sees product categories: Laptops, Monitors, Accessories."

**[00:11]** "They browse a category, see product cards with images, prices, and availability. A laptop shows: 1,199, in stock, 5-star rating."

**[00:21]** "They click into the product detail page. More specs, reviews, and an Add to Cart button."

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:28]** "Items land in the shopping cart. The buyer can adjust quantities, remove items, or add notes for their manager."

**[00:38]** "The cart sidebar shows totals, applied promotions, and a Request Quote button — or they can proceed directly to checkout."

**[00:48]** "At checkout, they confirm shipping address, review company billing info, and finalize the order."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:57]** "The order is placed. They see a confirmation with order number, expected delivery, and a PDF receipt."

**[01:05]** "Full e-commerce flow complete."
