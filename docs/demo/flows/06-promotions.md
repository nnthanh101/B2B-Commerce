# Flow 06: Promotions

**Persona**: Buyer  
**Scenario**: Automatic and manual promotions reduce cart total and improve buyer savings visibility.  
**Duration**: ~2 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "Promotions reward bulk buying. In the cart, the buyer sees their items and the subtotal: 3,500."

**[00:10]** "As items are added, a promotion triggers automatically: 'Bulk purchase promotion: 10% off orders over 2,000.'"

**[00:19]** "The discount is calculated and applied instantly. The new total is 3,150 — a savings of 350."

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:27]** "The buyer also sees a 'Free Shipping' badge — they've qualified because their order exceeds 1,000 in value."

**[00:35]** "Promotions can also be manual: the buyer can enter a promo code, like 'EARLYBIRD10' for an additional 10% off sale items."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:46]** "All promotions stack. The buyer sees a clear breakdown: subtotal, promotion discount, shipping, tax, and final total."

**[00:54]** "Promotions flow complete."
