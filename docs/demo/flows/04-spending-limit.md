# Flow 04: Spending Limit Enforcement

**Persona**: Buyer + Admin  
**Scenario**: The system enforces monthly spending limits at checkout to prevent budget overruns.  
**Duration**: ~2 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "Spending limits protect company budgets. Each employee has a monthly cap — ours is 50,000."

**[00:10]** "The buyer has already spent 30,000 this month. They try to add a 25,000 equipment bundle to their cart."

**[00:19]** "The cart shows: subtotal 25,000, but the system flags a warning: 'Exceeds spending limit. You have 20,000 remaining.'"

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:28]** "The purchase is blocked. The buyer has two options: reduce the order, or request an exception from their manager."

**[00:37]** "A manager can override the limit for critical needs, but it creates an audit trail — the system logs who approved the exception and why."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:47]** "This prevents the buyer from accidentally overspending. In this case, the buyer reduces the order to 15,000 and proceeds."

**[00:55]** "Spending limit enforcement complete."
