# Flow 05: Quote Negotiation

**Persona**: Buyer + Admin (Sales Manager)  
**Scenario**: Buyer and seller exchange messages to negotiate terms before the deal is finalized.  
**Duration**: ~2.5 min

## Script (voice narration)

**[00:00]** "Quote negotiation is where deals get refined. The buyer has requested a quote for 100 units of keyboards."

**[00:10]** "The seller reviews the request and sees a note: 'bulk discount expected' — the buyer wants a special price for volume."

**[00:19]** "The seller opens the Messages tab and types: 'For 100+ units, we can offer 15% off. New total: 8,500.'"

(HOLD 1 second...)

**[00:28]** "The buyer receives the counteroffer. They check their budget, accept the terms, and reply: 'Great. Please proceed with fulfillment setup.'"

**[00:38]** "Each exchange is timestamped and stored for audit purposes. No ambiguity about what was promised."

(HOLD 2 seconds...)

**[00:47]** "Once both sides agree, the quote moves to the next status: Ready for Order. The buyer and seller have a clear contract in text."

**[00:57]** "Quote negotiation complete."
