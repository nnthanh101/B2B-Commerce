# Flow 09: Bulk Add to Cart

**Persona**: Maria (Buyer-Employee)

**Scenario**: Maria has a spreadsheet of 18 SKUs with quantities for a quarterly office restock. She navigates to Bulk Order Pad, pastes the CSV (SKU, Qty), and the system resolves all SKUs in seconds, validates inventory, and adds them to her cart. No re-keying. No errors. No copy-paste mishaps. Cart is ready to quote or checkout in one paste.

**Status**: ✅ GREEN

**Duration**: ~2 min
