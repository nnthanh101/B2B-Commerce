# Flow 03: Company Management

**Persona**: David (Admin)

**Scenario**: David needs to add a new team member (Sarah) to the procurement platform and set her monthly spending limit to $2,000 NZD. He navigates to Company Settings, adds Sarah's email, sets her role (Employee) and limit, and saves. The system generates an invite token. Sarah receives the token, sets a password, and joins the company — all without David filing a support ticket or manually creating a user.

**Status**: ✅ GREEN

**Duration**: ~2 min
