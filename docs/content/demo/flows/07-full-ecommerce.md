# Flow 07: Full E-Commerce Flow

**Persona**: Maria (Buyer-Employee)

**Scenario**: Maria logs into the B2B storefront (not a catalog or quote engine — a real store). She browses product categories, filters by region (NZ), views prices in NZD, adds items to cart, reviews her remaining spending limit, and checks out. The order confirms and Maria receives a tracking number. No approval required. No quote step. Just pure self-service ecommerce, B2B style.

**Status**: ✅ GREEN

**Duration**: ~2 min
