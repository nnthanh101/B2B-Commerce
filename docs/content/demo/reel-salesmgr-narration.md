## Why this matters — Sales Manager lens

**Why**: A sales manager loses deals to negotiation latency — a week of email per counter-offer, context scattered across inboxes. This reel collapses that into one platform: Priya's queue flags the countered quote and the whole thread lives in one place.

**Business value**: The reel shows a real counter from NZ$1,582.00 to NZ$1,423.80 applied on the line item, a 2-message thread, and an in-platform Accept/Reject — the deal closes with the price history and every message on the record.

**What-if-missing**: Without in-platform negotiation, counter-offers scatter across email, pricing context is lost, and deals stall — the inverse of the two-messages-not-two-weeks outcome this reel demonstrates.
