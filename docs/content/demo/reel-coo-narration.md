## Why this matters — COO lens

**Why**: A COO measures procurement in throughput, not clicks. A quarterly restock that consumes an afternoon of manual lookups is operational drag this reel removes — Maria enters by SKU×quantity and pastes a repeat order directly.

**Business value**: The reel shows bulk entry, a quick-order paste pad, and CSV export resolving 3 lines to NZ$539 in minutes — and because that total is over Maria's limit, it still routes to Request Quote. Velocity without losing spend governance.

**What-if-missing**: Without bulk entry, paste-pad, and CSV, every restock reverts to a spreadsheet-and-afternoon workflow — slower cycle times and a governance gap if buyers route around the cart to move faster.
