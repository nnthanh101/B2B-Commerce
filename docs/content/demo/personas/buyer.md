# Persona: Maria (Buyer-Employee)

Maria is a procurement specialist at a mid-market manufacturing company. She spends 30+ hours per month managing purchase requests, negotiating with suppliers, and tracking order status. Her frustration: the old email-based quote process takes 3–5 days, and budget overruns slip through because she can't see her team's remaining spend in real time.

**What Maria cares about**: Speed to purchase (minutes, not days) and visibility into budget constraints before checkout.

**Key capability she unlocks**: One-click cart-to-quote, instant spending-limit feedback, bulk imports for recurring orders, and a full self-service ecommerce experience without support tickets.

## Flows Maria owns
- Flow 01: Cart to Quote
- Flow 04: Spending Limit
- Flow 06: Promotions
- Flow 07: Full Ecommerce
- Flow 09: Bulk Add
- Flow 10: Quick Order Pad
