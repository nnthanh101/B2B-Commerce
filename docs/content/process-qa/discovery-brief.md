## Executive Summary

B2B-Commerce is a **quote-assisted B2B-Commerce** built on Medusa 2.x for ANZ regulated-industry buyers (Energy, FSI, Telecom). The product replaces 6-week email-PDF procurement cycles with a deterministic Quote → Approval → PO → Invoice → SOW workflow.
